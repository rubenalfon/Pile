Pile is a simple and powerful document management app designed to help you organize, access, and share your personal files — all in one place.

Built around a flexible tag system called "piles", the app lets you categorize your documents the way you think, not the way folders force you to.

✦ KEY FEATURES

• Centralized document management — keep all your files in one place
• Smart tag system (piles) — organize documents your way
• Full PDF support — view and share documents seamlessly
• Built-in search — find any document in seconds
• Modern Material Design — clean, intuitive interface
• 100% offline — no internet connection required
• Privacy-first — your documents never leave your device

✦ DESIGNED FOR PRIVACY

Pile does not collect any personal data. No analytics. No tracking. No cloud uploads. Everything stays on your device, under your control.

✦ PERFECT FOR

• Students managing notes, assignments, and study materials
• Professionals organizing contracts, invoices, and reports
• Anyone who wants a clean, private way to keep documents organized

✦ BUILT WITH MODERN TECHNOLOGY

Pile is built using the latest Android development standards, including Jetpack Compose and Material Design, ensuring a smooth and reliable experience.
