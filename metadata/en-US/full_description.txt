<p>Pile is a simple and powerful document management app designed to help you organize, access, and share your personal files &mdash; all in one place.</p>

<p>Built around a flexible tag system called "piles", the app lets you categorize your documents the way you think, not the way folders force you to.</p>

<h2>✦ KEY FEATURES</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Centralized document management &mdash; keep all your files in one place</li>
  <li>Smart tag system (piles) &mdash; organize documents your way</li>
  <li>Full PDF support &mdash; view and share documents seamlessly</li>
  <li>Built-in search &mdash; find any document in seconds</li>
  <li>Modern Material Design &mdash; clean, intuitive interface</li>
  <li>100% offline &mdash; no internet connection required</li>
  <li>Privacy-first &mdash; your documents never leave your device</li>
</ul>

<h2>✦ DESIGNED FOR PRIVACY</h2>
<p>Pile does not collect any personal data. No analytics. No tracking. No cloud uploads. Everything stays on your device, under your control.</p>

<h2>✦ PERFECT FOR</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Students managing notes, assignments, and study materials</li>
  <li>Professionals organizing contracts, invoices, and reports</li>
  <li>Anyone who wants a clean, private way to keep documents organized</li>
</ul>

<h2>✦ BUILT WITH MODERN TECHNOLOGY</h2>
<p>Pile is built using the latest Android development standards, including Jetpack Compose and Material Design, ensuring a smooth and reliable experience.</p>
